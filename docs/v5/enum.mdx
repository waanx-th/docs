### depositStatus
* `0`&emsp;unknown
* `1`&emsp;toBeConfirmed
* `2`&emsp;processing
* `3`&emsp;success (this is final status reflected by the finalized deposit status. If a blockchain rollback occurs, the deposit will be assigned a 7XXXX status, which represents the finalized outcome of that rollback case.)
* `4`&emsp;deposit failed
* `7`&emsp;**Rollback processing**: A blockchain rollback occurred after the deposit had already been credited.
* `70011`&emsp;**Deposit rolled back**: A blockchain rollback occurred, and the deposit has been reversed. The credited funds have been deducted, and no final credit remains.
* `70012`&emsp;**Deposit remains successful after rollback review**: Although a blockchain rollback occurred, the deposit remains valid. After review, it was determined that no clawback or deduction is required.
* `70013`&emsp;**Deposit rollback faill**: The deposit was initially credited, but the backend system failed to process the blockchain rollback automatically. The case is pending manual review.
* `10011`&emsp;pending to be credited to funding pool
* `10012`&emsp;Credited to funding pool successfully
